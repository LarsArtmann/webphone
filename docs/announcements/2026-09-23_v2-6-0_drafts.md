## Draft B — v2.6.0 headline post

**webphone v2.6.0 — failures that leave evidence, surfaces that
respect your attention**

webphone is a single-binary Go unified-communications web app: the
proven SIP.js call island plus server-rendered Messages, Fax,
Voicemail, History, Contacts and Settings tabs on one page.

The theme of this train: every failure leaves durable, in-place
evidence — and the twenty small papercuts around it stop existing.

- **The failed bubble tells the truth**: a refused or dropped send now
  persists the provider's reason ON the message bubble (kind + detail
  disclosed on the failed row), and the retry affordance appears only
  where a retry can actually succeed — a provider refusal never offers
  a retry that would fail identically, a transport drop always does,
  with the drafted body prefilled. Double-submit is guarded at the
  form level; a self-addressed thread warns at intent time.
- **Six affordances, zero new surfaces**: hover a bubble for its full
  timestamp, jump-to-latest chip on the thread, missed-call badge on
  the nav, dial typeahead over your contacts, thread search (`?q=`),
  and an audio-output picker for headset/speaker switching mid-call.
- **Call cards speak**: state chips (ringing/established/ending) with
  reduced-motion-aware pulses and spoken transitions through the toast
  live region — en and de.
- **Bounded retention** (`retention_days`): a daily sweep deletes
  messages with attachments, fax jobs with documents, and the threads
  they empty — off by default, operator-set.
- **Optional CRM enrichment** (`crm.url`/`crm.token`): read-only name
  enrichment for panels and an idempotent post-call journal; a dead
  CRM never breaks a page, unknown numbers never mint contacts.
- **Hardening**: CSP with zero inline scripts end-to-end (theme
  preload included, verified by test), signed release tags, gzip'd
  text surfaces, backup retention with dated history + prune, and a
  duplicate-code budget driven to zero harmful clones.
- **Under the hood**: both dedup trains landed one-home helpers with
  their own contract pins, webhook status hooks share one idempotent
  tail (replays inert, unknown refs 404 so providers stop retrying,
  failures stay retryable), and the SIP island keeps its bounded
  watchdog + JsSIP named fallback posture.
