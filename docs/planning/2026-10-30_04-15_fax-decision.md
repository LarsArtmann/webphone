## Telnyx facts (as far as the repos record them; live-account checks were never possible)

- A Telnyx number binds to exactly ONE connection: a Programmable Fax
  application cannot share the voice DID `+1727289311` (FQDN
  connection). A future enablement needs a dedicated fax DID.
- The stack asserts `faxDid ≠ voice DID` (hosts/pbx-prod assertion).
- Outbound would be Telnyx `POST /faxes` + status webhooks
  (`fax.queued|media.processed|sending.started|delivered|failed`);
  inbound events `fax.receiving.started|fax.media.processing.started|
  fax.received (10-min signed media_url)|fax.failed`.
- Whether the SIP FQDN connection supports T.38 today is UNVERIFIED
  (vendor-reading only). The receive path is G.711 rxfax
  (`fax_use_t38=false`) and does not require T.38.
- Outbound calls are fraud-blocked on this datacenter IP (Telnyx 403,
  support ticket owed) — an outbound fax path would likely inherit that
  block; another reason "keep off" is the cheap state.
