00Summary

Webphone consumes the family deliberately: nine entries are direct dependencies (error-family, httputil + server_timing, go-sse, go-health, go-health-dashboard, templ-components, go-cqrs-lite, cqrs-htmx), four ride indirect-only (idempotency, retry, codec, etag — each with a written reason in its own report), and six are absent by decision (atomic-write, filewatcher, daemon, output, credentials-store, appkit). The scored families average 76/100; the audit found one genuine correctness gap (blob durability), one standing finding from the 2026-09-24 audit still open (MaxBodySize), and a handful of cheap wins.

The bottom line Adopt go-atomic-write for blob.Save — it is the only user-data durability hole found (attachments/fax PDFs written with bare os.WriteFile, no fsync/rename). Then retire the 12 hand-rolled MaxBytesReader sites for httputil.MaxBodySize (open since September). Everything else is either at its deliberate boundary or a reasoned decline.

02Findings — top opportunities (impact × ease)

Six actions, sorted by impact then ease. Each links to its evidence.

High impact · Low effort

Adopt go-atomic-write for blob persistence

internal/blob/store.go:50 · cmd/webphone/catalog.go:56,64 · cmd/webphone/contracts.go:36

The blob store saves attachments and fax PDFs with a bare os.WriteFile(abs, content, 0o640) — no fsync, no atomic rename. Write ordering verified file-then-row at all four call sites (T01 errata), so the precise window is row-committed-before-file-durable: a power loss after the database row commits can leave a truncated blob behind a row that references it: user data that 404s (inbound faxes: the only copy). This is the only user-data durability hole found across all 19 libraries. The CLI spec writers get WriteIfChanged (no torn diffs, no mtime churn) in the same move. Ride the vendorHash re-pin ritual in the same commit.

Fix atomicwrite.Write(abs, content) in blob.Save (content is already in memory), WriteIfChanged for the two CLI writers. Full analysis: go-atomic-write.md
Medium impact · Low effort

Retire the 12 hand-rolled MaxBytesReader sites for httputil.MaxBodySize

actions.go:652, transcribe.go:54, passkey_api.go:90,203, hooks/webhooks.go:425, api/*.go JSON guards

The standing finding #1 of the 2026-09-24 fleet audit — still open at v1.8.0. The library ships the middleware; twelve call sites re-implement it by hand. Mechanical migration, covered by the existing httpspec + per-route tests.

Medium impact · Low effort

templ-components cheap wins: StatusBadge, SimpleEmptyState, feedback.Alert

fax.templ:214, messages.templ:738-744 (badges); wp-empty ×10; error.templ

Status badges, ten inline empty-state paragraphs and the error banner are hand-rolled while the same panels already use the library’s Button and EmptyState — the inconsistency is the argument, not the count. Cascading-layer contract and greppable row classes must survive the swap. panelHead → PageHeader follows on the next panel-touching train.

Medium impact · Medium effort

Declare the two trim caps with metaengine EnforceCap

internal/readmodels/transcripts.go:106-130 (trimOwner/trimCall) · dialwindows.go:229 (trimWindows)

metaengine ships EnforceCap(name) (newest-rows trim) — exactly the shape webphone hand-rolls for transcripts (5000) and dial windows (500). Reject-caps (contacts, call reviews) must stay app-side on purpose: trimming would silently evict owner content, and that is a written contract.

Low-medium impact · Low effort

Close the three version gaps

go-sse v0.6.2→v0.7.0 · go-health v0.8.1→v0.9.0 · go-etag v0.6.1→v0.7.0 (with adoption)

go-health v0.9.0’s CoerceValidUTF8 is the substantive one: probe details embed driver strings that can carry non-UTF-8 bytes, and probe JSON must never break. go-sse v0.7.0 brings EventID branding + the ssetest breaking change in one mechanical train.

Low impact · Low effort

go-error-family: put 409-shaped outcomes in the Conflict family

internal/readmodels (note_list_full, dial-gate conflicts)

The Conflict family has zero uses; conflict-shaped outcomes map to Rejection today. One-family migration + family_test updates. (Retry-policy consumption rides the go-retry question — see its report for the shape mismatch.)
