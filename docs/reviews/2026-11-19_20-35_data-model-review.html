Disposition echoes journal no-change events

internal/cqrs/callnotes.go:134 (body lane drops same-body echoes) vs :162 (disposition lane journals unconditionally) · readmodels/notesgate.go:77

The autosave doctrine — the journal records CHANGES, not echoes — is enforced on the note-body lane and absent on the disposition lane. A re-click or second tab re-PUTting the same verdict journals a no-change event, bumps UpdatedAt (which History renders as recency), and fires an SSE nudge over an unchanged slot. The asymmetry is undocumented at both sites.

Fix (Option A, symmetry) One guard in decideSaveDisposition + a changed bool return at the gate, mirroring NotesGate.Set; the disposition-lane twin of TestNoteSameBodySaveIsNotARevision pins it. Effort XS.

06Validation layer — where invalid states become unrepresentable

The repo’s validation doctrine is parse at the door, carry the brand — and the eight fixes move three more validations into that shape:

  • From prose to parser (F4): the provider set is currently enforced by an error message enumerating it; the enum’s Parse becomes the validation, one step earlier, with the config wire untouched.
  • From post-hoc guard to structural join (F7): boot-time ParseExtension checks stop being a separate step the join must trust; the join consumes the parsed keys themselves.
  • From convention to constructor (F1, F6): vocabularies whose only home is a comment become constants; a typo’d literal stops compiling (F6, once its trigger fires) instead of silently dropping beacons.

What deliberately stays OUT of the type layer: wire DTO bytes (BD003 directives), journal fact fields (engine-row doctrine, frozen), foreign ids (client-generated device ids, agent draft ids), and DID formatting (display-verbatim by owner ruling).

08Anti-patterns — traps when adopting

  • Do not brand the wire. SessionIdentity, journal facts and config.js fields stay raw with their BD003/nolint directives; a branded type whose String() renders Brand: prefixes corrupts bytes every consumer pins.
  • Do not mint mechanical brands. The brandid-lint suggestions (FaxViewID, faxInputID…) split-brain the existing domain.FaxID-class brands; brands ride repo-owned identities and closed vocabularies only.
  • Do not move ThreadFlag to domain. Settled beside its only consumer (the gate); revisit only if a second consumer of the flag vocabulary appears.
  • Do not i18n service validation reasons and do not widen errorfamily.IsRetryable semantics while touching the chatloop backoff — both are adjacent, both are ruled.
  • Do not “fix” the six single-implementer Deps seams or the three edge doors — baseline-ruled (detector-blind classes), re-verified tonight.
  • Do not canonicalize DID. Verbatim is a product ruling; any normalization changes rendered bytes on configured deployments.
