- **Commit per verified file-batch, not per phase**: five daemon sweeps fragmented the narrative. Stage+commit in ONE call is the rule — this session proved the unit needs to be smaller when verification cycles are long.
- **Record every deliberate non-fix in the repo, not in the session**: the BuildFlow-binary-staleness and vendorHash-file-extraction declines live only here. The .buildflow.yml policy block is the home; two more short comments would have closed the loop properly.
- **Distinguish tool-policy rulings from tool-skip policy**: go-auto-upgrade's 68 suggestions need an OWNED ruling (adopt/decline) or they sit as permanent noise; "warning-severity, doesn't gate" is an observation, not a decision.
